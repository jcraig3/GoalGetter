    How it works

    One loop, from the data to the celebration.

    Data comes in. It becomes a metric. A goal is set on that metric. Progress is ranked and put on screen. Hitting the goal gets celebrated.

    1. 1

      Data in

      Upload a spreadsheet or connect a sheet, a database or a webhook. Agents never type in their own numbers, so the board is one people trust.

      CSV / ExcelSheetsSQLWebhook

      Measure

      Each row becomes a metric: revenue, calls, appointments, deals. Set goals for a person, a team, an office or the whole company, over any period.

    3. 3

      Motivate

      The TV wall

      Readable from across the room. Alive all day.

      The wall rotates through a podium, a race track, a goal gauge, head-to-heads and news. When someone hits a goal, every screen stops for a trophy, confetti and their walk-up song.

      • Big type and a dark background that won’t glare
      • TVs pair with a code, not someone’s login
      • A banner drops in the moment someone overtakes

      Built for every seat

      Everyone sees what they need, and nothing they shouldn’t.

      There are three roles plus the TV. The tour lets you switch between them at any time to see what each one sees.

      “Where do I stand, and what do I need today?”

      Agents see their own goals with pace, their rank on every board, the competitions they are in and the shout-outs they get. Their numbers come from your systems, never from a form.

      • Personal goals with an “expected by today” marker
      • Your own row pinned on every leaderboard
      • Alerts when someone passes you

      Your data, your servers

      Starts with a spreadsheet. Grows with your stack.

      GoalGetter runs inside your own network with one Docker command. A company with no CRM and no data warehouse can upload a spreadsheet and be live the same afternoon. Connectors make it faster, but you don’t need any of them to start.

      • Self-hosted: one company, one deployment, no subscription
      • Single sign-on or local accounts
      • Every admin correction is audited and visibly marked
